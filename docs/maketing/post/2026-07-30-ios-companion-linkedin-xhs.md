等遛完弯回来，Agent 的活儿也干完了，我再坐到电脑前验收。我不用再一直被拘在工位上了。

## LinkedIn (English)

A while back I shared termio.sh — a native Mac terminal for AI coding agents.
I've now built the iPhone side too.

The mobile app is for one thing: to let the agents keep working while I step
away. The work stays on my Mac, but I can go out for a walk with my family.

While I'm out on the walk, an agent might ask me "which of these approaches do
you want?" — I make the choice on my phone and let it carry on. I'm outside,
and the AI is still working for me. If an idea comes to me along the way, I can
hold to dictate it and tell the agent right away to start on it.

By the time I'm back from the walk, the agents are done, and I sit down at the
computer to review their work. I'm no longer tied to my desk.

The Mac app is free, no account. The iPhone companion is headed to the App
Store.
